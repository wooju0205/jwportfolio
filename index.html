About

A little more about how I work

I'm Jady Woo, a Computer Science student who spends most of my energy on the parts of software people actually touch — the layout, the copy, the micro-interaction that tells you something worked.

I like starting from the user's problem, not the component library: sketching flows, testing them in Figma, then building the real thing in React so nothing gets lost in translation between design and code.

I still enjoy the occasional dive into systems territory — I rewrote a process scheduler for a class OS this year — but my focus and my portfolio live on the frontend and product design side of the stack.

three things about me

  • I keep a paper sketchbook next to my laptop — bad UI ideas get killed there first, not in Figma
  • My favorite font pairing is a personality trait at this point, not a skill
  • I will absolutely rewrite a scheduler for fun on a weekend
